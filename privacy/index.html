3. How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiry. This is the main reason. If you submit a form, we contact you about it.
  • To qualify the enquiry. We ask about your business, what you need built and your timeline, so we can tell you honestly whether we are a fit.
  • To deliver work if you become a client — including support and account communication.

We do not sell your personal data, rent it, or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. WhatsApp messaging

Most of our contact with prospective clients happens on WhatsApp, so it is worth being specific.

  • When we message you. Only after you have submitted an enquiry form or messaged us first. We do not send cold WhatsApp messages to purchased or scraped lists.
  • Our first message uses a template approved by Meta and identifies AutoPend immediately.
  • An automated assistant may reply to you. Our WhatsApp replies are drafted by an AI assistant, described in section 5. It will tell you it is an assistant if you ask, and a human colleague can join the conversation at any point.
  • Stopping messages. Reply STOP at any time, or simply say you are not interested. We will mark the conversation closed and stop messaging you.
  • WhatsApp itself. Messages travel through the WhatsApp Business Platform, operated by Meta. Meta processes them under its own terms and privacy policy, which we do not control.

5. Automated processing and AI

We want to be plain about this, because many companies are not.

When you message us on WhatsApp, the content of your messages is sent to Google's Gemini language model, which drafts a reply and extracts a short summary of what you need — such as your industry, what you want built, and your timeline. That summary is stored alongside your contact record so our team can pick up the conversation with context.

What this means in practice:

  • Your messages leave our systems and are processed by Google as a service provider.
  • The assistant has access to the earlier messages in your conversation, so it does not ask you the same thing twice. It cannot see other people's conversations.
  • No decision with a legal or similarly significant effect on you is made by the AI. It drafts messages and organises notes. Decisions about pricing, whether to take on work, and what we agree to are made by people.
  • You can ask to speak to a human at any time, and we will hand the conversation over.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access — ask what personal data of yours we hold and how we use it.
  • Correction — have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected or completed.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data, unless we are legally required to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, as easily as you gave it. Reply STOP on WhatsApp, or email us.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint with us and receive a response.
  • Nominate — nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these, email kartikeygupta8@gmail.com with the phone number or email address you used to contact us, so we can find your record. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Security

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals as the DPDP Act requires.
