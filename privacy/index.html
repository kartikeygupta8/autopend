Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data AutoPend collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers our website, our WhatsApp messaging, and the enquiry forms we run on Facebook and Instagram.

The short version. If you fill in one of our enquiry forms, we get your name, phone number and email so we can contact you about the work you asked about. We reply to you on WhatsApp, and those replies are written by a person, not an AI. We use analytics to understand how our website is used. We do not sell your data. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

AutoPend builds websites, WhatsApp automation, AI voice agents and ERP/CRM software for businesses in India. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), AutoPend is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described in this policy — meaning we decide why and how it is processed.

3. How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiry. This is the main reason. If you submit a form, we contact you about it.
  • To qualify the enquiry. We ask about your business, what you need built and your timeline, so we can tell you honestly whether we are a fit.
  • To deliver work if you become a client — including support and account communication.
  • To improve our marketing. Aggregate analytics tell us which pages and adverts work. This is statistical, not about you individually.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax and accounting records.

We do not sell your personal data, rent it, or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. WhatsApp messaging

Most of our contact with prospective clients happens on WhatsApp, so it is worth being specific.

  • When we message you. Only after you have submitted an enquiry form or messaged us first. We do not send cold WhatsApp messages to purchased or scraped lists.
  • Our first message identifies AutoPend immediately and refers to the enquiry you made.
  • Who replies to you. A person from our team reads your message and writes the reply. We do not use an AI assistant to draft or send our WhatsApp messages, as section 5 sets out.
  • Stopping messages. Reply STOP at any time, or simply say you are not interested. We will mark the conversation closed and stop messaging you.
  • WhatsApp itself. Messages are sent and received through WhatsApp, operated by Meta. Meta processes them under its own terms and privacy policy, which we do not control.

5. Automated processing and AI

We want to be plain about this, because many companies are not.

We build AI systems for our clients, but we do not point one at your enquiry. The content of your messages to us is not sent to any AI or language-model provider. When you message us on WhatsApp, a person reads it and writes the reply, and the notes we keep about your enquiry are written by that person.

What this means in practice:

  • Your messages are not processed by an AI service provider, and are not used to train anyone's model.
  • The only automated processing of your data is the website analytics and advertising measurement described in section 6, which is about site traffic rather than the content of your conversation with us.
  • No decision with a legal or similarly significant effect on you is made automatically. Decisions about pricing, whether to take on work, and what we agree to are made by people.
  • If we ever introduce an AI assistant into our own messaging, we will update this section and the date at the top before we do, as section 14 describes.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access — ask what personal data of yours we hold and how we use it.
  • Correction — have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected or completed.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data, unless we are legally required to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, as easily as you gave it. Reply STOP on WhatsApp, or email us.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint with us and receive a response.
  • Nominate — nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these, email kartikeygupta8@gmail.com with the phone number or email address you used to contact us, so we can find your record. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) across our website and systems, encrypted storage of credentials and access tokens, access limited to people who need it, and hosting with established providers.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals as the DPDP Act requires.

12. Children
