Questions

Before you decide.

Is this the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. We use the official WhatsApp Business Platform, so your number is not at risk of a ban the way unofficial tools are.

What does the plan include?

Setup by us, the AI agent that replies, asks your questions and books the call, text and voice notes in any language, and WhatsApp charges. Broadcasts you send to your list are ₹1.50 each.

Does the AI make mistakes?

It only answers from what we train it on, and it hands over to your team when it is unsure. You can read every chat and switch the AI off per lead.

Will leads get messages I did not approve?

Meet links only go out after you press Send in the console. Follow-ups use pre-approved templates with a limit per lead.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Use the live demo on this site, then book a free 30-minute call and we will show it on your own business.

30 minutes. Free. No obligation. We will tell you honestly what is worth building.
