Before You Visit

Questions, answered.

How do I schedule a site visit?

Use the “Book a Site Visit” button, choose a residence, date and time, and an advisor will confirm on WhatsApp.

What configurations are available?

2, 3 and 4 BHK residences across our current projects in Gurugram, Noida and New Gurgaon.

Can I request a floor plan?

Yes — floor plans are shared after a quick enquiry so we can send the configuration that fits.

How does the enquiry process work?

Enquire on the site or WhatsApp, get matched with an advisor, then book a visit whenever it suits you.

Can I speak to an advisor on WhatsApp?

Yes — every enquiry gets an instant WhatsApp response, with a human advisor available on request.
