Questions

Before you decide.

Will people know it is an AI?

The agent introduces itself as an AI assistant from your business. Honesty up front gets better outcomes and keeps you compliant.

Can it call people on DND?

No. We follow TRAI rules, and it only calls leads who enquired with you and asked to be contacted.

What if the lead asks something it cannot answer?

It says it will have a team member follow up, notes the question and sends it to you on WhatsApp.

How accurate is Hindi?

Good for everyday business Hindi and Hinglish. We test it on your own words and tune the script before launch.

How many minutes will I use?

A typical qualifying call is 1 to 3 minutes, so 1,000 minutes covers roughly 400 to 600 calls.

Ready for a caller that never sleeps?

30 minutes. Free. No obligation. We will tell you honestly what is worth building.
